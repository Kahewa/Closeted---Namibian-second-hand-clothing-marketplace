How to buy

Find it, love it, WhatsApp it.

No accounts, no checkout forms. Pick the pieces you love and send them to us on WhatsApp — we'll sort out the rest with you.

Shop pre-loved

How it works

  1. 1

    Browse the shop

    Scroll the feed or tap a category at the top (Dresses, Shoes, Tops…) to narrow it down.

  2. 2

    Take a closer look

    Tap any piece to see all its photos, the size, brand and condition, and similar pieces you might like.

  3. 3

    Buy now, or add to cart

    Want just that one? Tap Buy now and WhatsApp opens with the piece already in the message. Shopping for a few? Tap Add to cart on each.

  4. 4

    Send your cart on WhatsApp

    Open your cart (the bag icon, top right) and tap Send cart on WhatsApp. The message lists your pieces and the total, with a link so we see exactly what you picked.

  5. 5

    We take it from there

    We'll reply on WhatsApp to confirm your pieces and arrange payment and delivery with you.

Good to know

Every piece is pre-loved and one of a kind, so there's only one of each. Pieces marked Sold have already found a new home.
