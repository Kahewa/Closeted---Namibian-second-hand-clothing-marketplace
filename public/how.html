How Closet Drops Work

Whether you are buying curated fashion or clearing out your entire wardrobe, everything happens directly on WhatsApp with zero middlemen.

01

Snap & Build

Photograph your pre-loved clothes, shoes, and accessories. Set your prices in Namibian Dollars (N$), note the sizes, and add your WhatsApp number.

02

Pay Flat N$150

Pay one simple flat fee of N$150 via Bank Transfer, Pay2Cell, or EasyWallet. Add as many pieces to your carousel as you like — no hidden cuts!

03

Get Paid on WhatsApp

Your drop goes live on the feed. Namibian buyers swipe through, click your WhatsApp button, and arrange instant payment and pickup directly with you.

✦ Step-by-Step Seller Guide

Sell Your Entire Closet

Post your whole wardrobe clear-out in one go. One flat fee of N$150 covers your entire carousel drop, however many pieces you list.

01

Claim Your @Username

Pick a unique username — it becomes your storefront link (e.g. profile.html?u=yourname) and your official payment reference code.

02

Build Your Carousel Drop

Upload your pre-loved clothing, shoes, and accessories. Set your price in NAD, select condition (New with tags, Like new, Good, Fair), and add your WhatsApp number.

03

Pay Flat N$150 Fee

Pay once per drop via FNB, Standard Bank, Pay2Cell, or EasyWallet. No per-item fees and zero commission cuts from what you earn.

04

Fast Moderation

Our team checks your payment reference and activates your drop. It immediately appears on the homepage feed for shoppers across Namibia!

05

Manage & Mark Sold

As buyers message you and send money on WhatsApp, click Mark Sold on your card to stamp items out. Delete or archive anytime!

✧ Step-by-Step Buyer Guide

Shop Verified Wardrobes

01

Browse Real Closets

Swipe through carousel cards on the homepage feed or search by seller handle to find styles that fit you.

02

Check Details & Size

See high-res photos, condition tags, exact size measurements, and transparent pricing in Namibian Dollars (N$).

03

04

Pay & Deliver

Agree on your preferred courier (Nampost, Hardap, local delivery) or local collection directly with the seller!
