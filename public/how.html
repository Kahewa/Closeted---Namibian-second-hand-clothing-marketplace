How It Works

Post your whole wardrobe clear-out in one go. One flat fee, however many pieces you list, and buyers message you straight on WhatsApp. No commission, no shipping, and nobody holding your money. Selling here is by invitation, so the first step is getting your link from us.

N$150 per closet

For Sellers

  1. Ask us for an invite Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you a link. Only invited sellers can make an account, which keeps the closets real.
  2. Set up your account Open your link and pick a username. It's how buyers find you, and it's the reference you'll pay with. Your real name is optional.
  3. Add a social link Instagram, TikTok or Facebook. Buyers want to see who they're buying from, so a WhatsApp number on its own isn't enough to post.
  4. Build your closet Add as many pieces as you like. One photo each, with size, brand, condition and price.
  5. Pay the N$150 fee One flat fee for the whole closet, however many items it holds. Pay by bank transfer, wallet or cash.
  6. Wait for approval We match your payment against your reference, then your closet goes live automatically.
  7. Edit until it's approved Change anything while it's pending. Once it's live, the listing is locked.
  8. Mark pieces sold Cross items off as they go, and take the whole closet down whenever you like.
