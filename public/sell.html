How it works

  1. 1

    Fill in the form

    Tell us your details and roughly how many pieces you want to sell.

  2. 2

    We message you on WhatsApp

    We will reach out to confirm your details and arrange a time for pickup.

  3. 3

    Get your items ready

    Make sure your pieces are washed and ironed. We will come pick them up and take the photos for you.

  4. 4

    We list and sell

    We market your items and keep them in our shop until they sell, or until you ask for your unsold pieces back.

  5. 5

    Get paid

    Payouts happen on the 15th and 30th of every month. You can also message us anytime to check if your items have sold and request your money early.
